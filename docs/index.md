# LabNotesAI — AI‑Augmented Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio. I’m a senior software engineer who works with AI as a development partner — not a replacement.  
This space documents the real process behind my projects: the experiments, the debugging, the prompt refinement, the multi‑model workflows, and the lessons learned along the way.
